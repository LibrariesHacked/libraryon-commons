---
question: Can I renew a library digital loan?
source_url: https://libraryon.org/reading/e-lending-in-libraries
attribution: Originally published by LibraryOn / The British Library. CC BY 4.0
  except where otherwise noted; retain individual credits and rights notices.

Yes, you can renew e-book and audiobook loans through your app. If someone else has it on hold you might only be allowed to renew a few times.

You don't need to renew digital newspaper and magazines, as there is no return date for these.
