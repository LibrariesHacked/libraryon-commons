---
question: Can I renew loans of digital newspapers and e-magazines?
source_url: https://libraryon.org/reading/e-lending-in-libraries
attribution: Originally published by LibraryOn / The British Library. CC BY 4.0
  except where otherwise noted; retain individual credits and rights notices.
---

Unlike e-books and audiobooks, digital newspapers and magazines have no loan period – so you don’t need to return them.
