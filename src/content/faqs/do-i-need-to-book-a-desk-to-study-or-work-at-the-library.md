---
question: Do I need to book a desk to study or work at the library?
source_url: https://libraryon.org/work-and-skills/study-work-spaces
attribution: Originally published by LibraryOn / The British Library. CC BY 4.0

Most libraries with desks offer them on a first-come-first-served basis. That means you don’t need to book them – but you might need to be quick to get one.
