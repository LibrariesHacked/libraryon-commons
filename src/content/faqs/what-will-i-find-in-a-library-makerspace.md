Things you might find in a library makerspace include:

- 3D printers
- Photo, video and animation production equipment
- Podcasting studios or equipment
- Sewing machines
- Laser cutters
- Virtual Reality headsets
- Robotics systems
- Code clubs
- Woodworking or metalworking equipment
- Screen printing tools.
