Most makerspaces in libraries have been designed to appeal to people of all ages. Some of the technology might only be suitable for adults though.

People use makerspaces to do things related to their hobbies or their work, such as if they've started their own business. They might use a makerspace to:

- Create or edit content, such as photography or video
- Make 3D-printed prototypes of products they're working on
- Practise traditional arts and crafts like woodworking, sewing or painting
- Learn about creating through coding, electronics and robotics.

Every makerspace is different. If a library near you has one, it's a good idea to drop by and chat to the staff to learn more.
