**Arts Council England (ACE)** is the national development agency for creativity and culture. Since 2011, when it assumed responsibilities from the former Museums, Libraries and Archives Council (MLA), Arts Council England has been the development agency for public libraries in England.

ACE administers the **Libraries Improvement Fund (LIF)**, part of the Cultural Investment Fund, which supports local authorities in upgrading public library buildings and digital technology.
