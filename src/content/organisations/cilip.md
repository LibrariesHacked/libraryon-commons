**CILIP (The Chartered Institute of Library and Information Professionals)** is the UK's library and information association. Operating under Royal Charter, CILIP represents information professionals and public librarians across the country.

CILIP advocates for public library funding, defends intellectual freedom and against book censorship, and sets professional accreditation and ethical standards for the sector.
