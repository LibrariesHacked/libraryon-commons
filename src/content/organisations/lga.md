The **Local Government Association (LGA)** is the national membership body for local authorities in England and Wales, representing over 300 councils.

The LGA champions the vital role that council-run public libraries play in literacy, community cohesion, digital inclusion, health and wellbeing, and economic recovery. It lobbies central government for sustainable long-term funding for local services and shares best practice among portfolio holders and lead councillors.
