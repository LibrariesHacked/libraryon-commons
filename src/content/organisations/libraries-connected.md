**Libraries Connected** (formerly the Society of Chief Librarians, SCL) is an independent charity and membership organisation representing public library services across England, Wales, and Northern Ireland.

It serves as the Sector Support Organisation for libraries designated by Arts Council England, championing the role of libraries in literacy, health, digital inclusion, and culture through its **Universal Library Offers**.
