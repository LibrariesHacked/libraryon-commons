**Local authorities** (unitary authorities, metropolitan boroughs, London boroughs, county councils, and district/city councils) are the statutory bodies legally responsible for providing public library services across the UK.

In England and Wales, under the **Public Libraries and Museums Act 1964**, every library authority has a legal duty to provide a "comprehensive and efficient" library service for all persons desiring to make use thereof. In Scotland, statutory duties stem from the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, and in Northern Ireland, public library provision is centralised through Libraries NI under the Libraries Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

Local authorities fund library buildings, employ professional library staff, curate book and digital collections, offer community events, and adapt modern services to meet community needs.
