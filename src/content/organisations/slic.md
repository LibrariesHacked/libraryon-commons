The **Scottish Library and Information Council (SLIC)** is the independent advisory body to the Scottish Government on library and information services across Scotland.

SLIC develops Scotland's national public library strategy, manages the Scottish Government's **Public Library Improvement Fund (PLIF)**, and supports digital innovation, school libraries, and health and literacy initiatives across Scotland's 32 councils.
