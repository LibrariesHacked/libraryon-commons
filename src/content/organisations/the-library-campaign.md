**The Library Campaign** is an independent national charity (established in 1984) dedicated solely to supporting and defending publicly funded, statutory public library services.

Working closely with local campaign groups and supporting **Public Libraries News**, The Library Campaign supports grassroots communities facing library closures, funding reductions, or volunteer handovers.
