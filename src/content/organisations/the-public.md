**The public** are the cornerstone of the public library system. Libraries are uniquely universal civic institutions—open to everyone, regardless of age, background, income, or belief.

Members of the public interact with libraries in countless ways:
- **Readers & Learners**: Borrowing books, downloading audiobooks and eBooks, researching family history, and using quiet study spaces.
- **Children & Families**: Attending baby rhyme times, children's reading clubs, and homework support sessions.
- **Jobseekers & Entrepreneurs**: Accessing free Wi-Fi, computer suites, printing, and Business & IP Centres.
- **Community Groups & Volunteers**: Meeting in community spaces, running reading groups, and volunteering alongside library staff.
- **Citizens & Campaigners**: Championing local branches, participating in public consultations, and defending statutory services.
