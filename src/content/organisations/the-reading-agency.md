**The Reading Agency** is a UK charity dedicated to helping people become confident readers. Working in close partnership with public library services, the organisation reaches millions of readers each year.

Flagship programmes include the annual **Summer Reading Challenge** (reaching around 700,000 children in libraries each summer), **Reading Well** (books on prescription for mental health and dementia), and **Quick Reads**.
