The **Welsh Government (Llywodraeth Cymru)** holds devolved responsibility for public libraries across the 22 local authorities in Wales.

Under the Public Libraries and Museums Act 1964 as it applies to Wales, the Welsh Ministers assess local authority library performance using the statutory **Welsh Public Library Standards (WPLS)** framework and support capital developments through the Culture and Sport transformation fund.
