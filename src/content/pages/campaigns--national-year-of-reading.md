# If you're into it, read into it

National Year of Reading

Interested in gaming? Or sports? Or gardening? Your local library is the perfect place to explore it.

Browse more online, meet others who share the same passions. Why not join a class? Or listen to an audiobook? You can even borrow a librarian to chat to (but you do have to return them afterwards). Or, you might just want to find the right read. You can get all of this in your library, all for free, in your community.

## Looking for reading recommendations?

Have a hobby you want to deep dive into? Not sure what you like to read? Want something a bit different? Each month on Instagram, we pull together book recommendations from library staff around the country, each with a different theme. If your TBR pile is looking a little sad, add some of these to your list!
