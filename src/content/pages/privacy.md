# Privacy policy

LibraryCommons is an independent community project maintained by [Libraries Hacked](https://www.librarieshacked.org/). We are committed to protecting your privacy and operating with data minimisation at the core of everything we build.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver this public archive, directory, and community platform.

## Summary of principles

- **No user accounts or tracking:** We do not require you to create an account or log in to browse the site. We do not use advertising, marketing, or cross-site tracking cookies.
- **Search privacy:** When you search for libraries by town or postcode, or use device location, coordinates are processed securely to return nearby results and are never saved or tied to your personal identity.
- **Open data & transparency:** The directory contains public-sector, non-personal data about public library services and buildings.
- **Open-source:** All code and data pipelines for this website are open-source and publicly inspectable on [GitHub](https://github.com/LibrariesHacked/libraryon-commons).

## What information we process

### 1. Library directory searches

When using the library search on [Find a library](/libraries/):

- **Place and postcode lookups:** If you type a town or UK postcode to search for libraries, the query is resolved in your browser using local search indexes or secure HTTPS lookups. Postcodes and search terms are not stored, recorded, or linked to individual users.
- **Device location ("Find nearby"):** If you explicitly choose to use the "Find nearby" button, your browser requests your location coordinates via standard browser geolocation permissions. These coordinates are used solely within your current browser session to calculate distances to nearby libraries. Your location is never sent to tracking servers or stored by LibraryCommons.

### 2. Website hosting and technical logs

The static site is hosted on GitHub Pages (with content management administration hosted via Netlify):

- Standard web traffic logs (including your IP address, browser user-agent, and request timestamps) may be recorded by the hosting infrastructure providers for security, operational reliability, and DDoS prevention, in line with [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) and [Netlify's Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).
- LibraryCommons does not combine hosting logs with any personal profile data.

### 3. Cookies and local storage

- **No analytics or advertising cookies:** LibraryCommons does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or other third-party tracking scripts.
- **Functional storage only:** The site may use standard browser session storage or local storage exclusively to preserve your chosen search filters or active tabs during your visit. These do not track you across the web.

### 4. Community contributions and contact

- **Public contributions:** If you contribute to LibraryCommons by submitting a pull request, opening an issue, or saving an edit through the content management system, your GitHub username, git commits, and public comments become part of the public version control history in the repository.
- **Direct communication:** If you contact us via email, we use your email address and message details only to reply to your enquiry and address your feedback.

## Third-party links

LibraryCommons includes links to external websites, including official local authority websites, library catalogues, mapping services (Google Maps, Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap), and cultural partner organisations. We have no control over and are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies when visiting them.

## Your rights and contacting us

Under UK data protection laws (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), you have rights regarding any personal information that may be processed, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this privacy policy or data handling on LibraryCommons, please contact:

- **Email:** [info@librarieshacked.org](mailto:info@librarieshacked.org)
- **Project repository:** [LibrariesHacked/libraryon-commons on GitHub](https://github.com/LibrariesHacked/libraryon-commons)
