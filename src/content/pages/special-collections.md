## **Work in progress**

Libraries across the country preserve items that help people understand local history and culture. These items are often grouped under the term 'special collections'.

We've created this section because we think it might help demonstrate this part of what libraries do, and encourage people to explore special collections at libraries.

We are actively seeking feedback to help us decide on future investment in this strand of work. Should we produce more stories from library special collections? What kind of stories would you like to see?
