# Business support in libraries

> "The seminars and one-to-one sessions on marketing, intellectual property and business coaching were incredibly valuable."

Ben Francoise

Founder, Saints Coffee

## Business & IP Centres in libraries

You can find British Library [Business & IP Centres](https://www.bl.uk/hubs/business) (BIPCs) in more than 70 public libraries across the UK.

They support **over 40,000 people** every year to start and grow their businesses with **free, impartial support**.

They offer invaluable training and networking opportunities, and offer the UK's largest collection of business and intellectual property (IP) information.

**Did you know?**

90% of businesses that start with the support of a Business & IP Centre are still trading after three years (the national average is 50%).

## How BIPC helped Ben’s business

Ben founded [Saints Coffee](https://www.saintscoffee.co.uk/), a social enterprise coffee chain.

He did it with the help of [BIPC Northamptonshire](https://www.bipcnorthamptonshire.co.uk/), which is based in [Northamptonshire Central Library](/libraries/west-northamptonshire/northamptonshire-central-library).
