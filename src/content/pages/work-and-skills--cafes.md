# Cafes in libraries

## Meet, work and unwind

Take a break from studying, strategising or storytime at the library. **Lots of libraries offer cafes** where you can chat with friends or take a breather after an activity.

They’re a great alternative to high street coffee chains, and you know spending money there goes towards a great cause: keeping a library running.

Whether you’re building a business, a child’s vocabulary or a study plan, libraries are also places to build in a moment of relaxation.

![The counter of a well-stocked modern-looking cafe in a library, offering a wide range of drinks and snacks.](/uploads/archive/7de54345adc8ea84039ccf8706ae99afb94986372467f027c0e6ea9c5c4606f5.jpg)
