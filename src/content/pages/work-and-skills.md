# Work and skills in libraries

![People talk with a business advisor in a public library business support session. In the background other library users use computers.](/uploads/archive/63cf1714f14ca2ee1357f8ab47ad3a477e8932a76e617406756387ff504d11ea.jpg)

### [Business support in libraries](/work-and-skills/business-support)

British Library Business & IP Centres help over 40,000 people every year start, grow and protect their businesses.

![A woman in a library makerspace sits in front of a podcast microphone wearing headphones.](/uploads/archive/74ba883bcba2b1e80c6c8c6b8c68b58108dab6d8e65cea47aa3f27b496a29f1a.png)

### [Makerspaces in libraries](/work-and-skills/makerspaces)

Find your voice in a podcast studio, or have a go with 3D printers. Find coding clubs for kids and spaces where anyone can learn the skills to become a maker.

![A group of people chat while sittingaround a table in a library cafe.](/uploads/archive/6ef6eb66a2d52aa9e12d8ef45b0ba81ceac0331081e79c9236d465911cc46774.jpg)

### [Cafes in libraries](/work-and-skills/cafes)

Many libraries have cafes where you can meet friends, relax after an event or study somewhere different.

![A man wearing wireless headphones works on a laptop at a library desk.](/uploads/archive/2c8b56d5aa28082f971835620cd0763254f6eceac567af26b7ed12a6e028e35e.jpg)

### [Study and work spaces in libraries](/work-and-skills/study-work-spaces)

Need a free, safe space with reliable Wi-Fi? The library is the perfect alternative to noisy cafés or working from home.

![Two people using tablets and laptops together during a library digital skills session](/uploads/archive/819325e7e66d6f64e51ea0f65f93782d01ea497e3d5df13e3601e7d7a60d7cff.jpg)

### [Digital skills in libraries](/work-and-skills/digital-skills)

Delivering the skills we all need to make the most of today's tech. Attend free drop-ins to learn how to use your devices and the internet safely.
